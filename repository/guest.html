♙ Authors: Clarisse C. Banua, Onidranreb R. Bernardino, Rojhen D. Cleope (2024)

Abstract

This study examines the effects of scheduling Medical-Surgical Nursing courses on the learning dynamics of nursing students from the Bicol University West and Tabaco Campuses. It explores how class schedules, clinical-duty arrangements, study periods, and workload distribution influence students' academic engagement, participation, and overall learning experience.

The findings identify scheduling practices that support effective learning while reducing conflicts between classroom instruction and clinical exposure. The study may guide nursing administrators and faculty members in developing balanced schedules that promote student well-being, improve knowledge retention, and strengthen clinical preparedness.
